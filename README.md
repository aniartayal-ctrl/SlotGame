# iGaming & Pragmatic Play Intelligence

Interactive research brief on the global iGaming market with a focus on **Pragmatic Play** — geographic footprint, popular games & features, blind spots, and competitor positioning.

## Open the report

Open [`report/index.html`](report/index.html) in a browser (charts load via Chart.js CDN).

## Contents

1. Global online gambling market size & regional GGR mix  
2. Where Pragmatic Play leads (and where it doesn’t) — Blask lobby share  
3. Flagship games (Olympus, Bonanza, Big Bass, etc.)  
4. Popular mechanics & feature trends  
5. Blind spots (US, Australia, crash, live, ultra-vol niche)  
6. Competitor radar vs Evolution, Hacksaw, PG Soft, Spribe, RTG  

## Data

Structured source notes: [`data/research-sources.json`](data/research-sources.json)

Figures synthesize public industry reporting (Grand View Research, Track360, Blask, etc.). Lobby content share ≠ GGR. Methodologies differ across publishers.
