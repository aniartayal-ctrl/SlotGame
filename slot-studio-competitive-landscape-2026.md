# Online Slot Content Providers — Competitive Landscape Benchmarked Against Pragmatic Play

**Research date:** 29 July 2026
**Scope:** B2B online slot (RNG casino) game studios supplying regulated and grey-market online casino operators.
**Method:** Primary sources (audited annual reports, interim reports, SEC/LSE filings, regulator statements) preferred. Secondary industry data (Eilers & Krejcik, aggregator disclosures, lobby-scraper indices) used where no primary data exists. Every figure is tagged for confidence.

---

## 0. How to read this report — verification tiers

Every number in this report carries one of four tags. **Do not plot Tier C or Tier D figures without a caveat label.**

| Tag | Meaning | Examples in this report |
|---|---|---|
| **[HARD]** | Audited/regulator-filed or company-published financial statement figure from a primary source | Evolution FY2025 revenue, Hacksaw Q2 2026 revenue, L&W SEC filings, Playtech LSE RNS |
| **[FIRM]** | Company-published operational figure (not audited, but first-party and on the record) | Evolution "113 games released in 2025", Pragmatic Play "100+ network games", Games Global SEC F-1 operational KPIs |
| **[EST]** | Third-party research estimate with a named, credible methodology | Eilers & Krejcik GGR share, H2 Gambling Capital market sizing, SlotsRank lobby footprint |
| **[WEAK]** | Unverified, self-reported without audit, or from a low-quality/AI-generated secondary source. Directionally useful at best. | Spribe's player counts and valuation, "Pragmatic Play $1bn+ revenue", most private-studio revenue estimates |

**Things I could NOT verify and will not fabricate:**
- Pragmatic Play's revenue, profit, EBITDA or valuation. No audited accounts exist in any public registry. Any number you see quoted anywhere is an estimate.
- Play'n GO group consolidated revenue. Only Swedish subsidiary filings are public; the operating group is Maltese and does not file publicly.
- PG Soft, Jili, TaDa Gaming standalone revenue (parent IGS reports at group level only).
- Games Global FY2024, FY2025 or FY2026 revenue. Last disclosure was the withdrawn IPO prospectus (FY ending March 2023).
- Relax Gaming, Push Gaming, Thunderkick, Print Studios, Peter & Sons, Massive Studios standalone revenue.
- Greentube's revenue as a separate line inside Novomatic.
- Any single authoritative "global slot studio market share by GGR" table. It does not exist publicly. Section 4 assembles the best available proxies.

## 6. Cross-cutting analysis

### 6.1 The three competitive axes are not the same, and studios pick one

| Axis | Metric | Winner | Runners-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distribution scale** | Sites, countries, lobby positions | **Pragmatic Play** (514 sites, 45 countries) | Playtech (503), Games Global (483) |
| **Per-release quality** | Slots Launch avg score/game | **ELK Studios (206.4)** | Peter & Sons (195.0), Nolimit City (179.0), BTG (146.5) |
| **Profitability** | EBIT/EBITDA margin | **Hacksaw (82% adj. EBIT)** | Evolution (66% adj. EBITDA), ELK entity (~47% pre-tax), L&W iGaming (37% AEBITDA), Playtech B2B (20.5%) |

Pragmatic Play's average score per release is **25.13** — one-eighth of ELK's. It has **zero gold-rated releases in 2026 to date** against ELK's five and Nolimit City's three. Pragmatic wins on distribution and franchise gravity, not on individual game quality. That is a deliberate and, so far, correct strategic choice: it holds 17.8% European GGR share while ELK holds a fraction of that.

### 6.2 Margin structure separates platform businesses from content businesses

| Company | Margin metric | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| **Hacksaw Gaming** | Adjusted EBIT | **82%** |
| Nolimit City (at 2022 acquisition) | EBITDA | ~77% |
| **Evolution** | Adjusted EBITDA | **66.1%** |
| ELKAB Studios AB | Pre-tax | ~47% |
| **Light & Wonder iGaming** | AEBITDA | **37%** |
| EveryMatrix (group) | EBITDA | ~52–56% |
| **Playtech B2B** | Adjusted EBITDA | **20.5%** |

The spread from 82% to 20.5% is not about operational quality. It is about **where the cost of distribution sits.** Hacksaw and Nolimit City build a game once and push it through pre-existing integrations at zero marginal cost. Playtech carries platform, PAM, live-studio and account-management costs; Evolution carries 22,475 employees and 24 physical studios. **When benchmarking Pragmatic Play, note that it runs live studios in five countries and is expanding to nine — it is structurally a Playtech/Evolution-shaped cost base, not a Hacksaw-shaped one.**

### 6.3 The three structural shifts of 2025–2026

**1. Live casino has stopped growing and RNG has restarted.**
Evolution's Live revenue fell 3.1% (Q1 2026) and 3.6% (Q2 2026) year-on-year while RNG grew 8.1% then **14.0% — its first double-digit quarter in roughly three years.** Light & Wonder **exited live dealer entirely in 2025**. The live-casino land grab is over; slots are where growth has returned.

**2. Regulatory drag is now a first-order P&L item, and it hits suppliers, not just operators.**
- Evolution: ring-fencing "had a material impact on this year's performance"; a **£4.75m UKGC settlement** with the regulator stating it **considered licence suspension**; a mandated independent audit; Europe player-IP revenue down from €201.8m (Q4'24) to €173.0m (Q2'26), a 14% decline in six quarters.
- Playtech: Colombia VAT on deposits and Brazil transition effects contributed to a 27% LatAm decline.
- FDJ United: >€50m of gaming tax increases, revenue −3% on a restated basis.
- A new UK gambling tax regime took effect in **April 2026** with expected knock-on effects down the supply chain.

**3. Consolidation has removed most of the public comparables.**
Five suppliers left public markets in 12 months (NeoGames, IGT in its prior form, Everi, PlayAGS, GAN). Snaitech moved from Playtech to Flutter for €2.3bn. Kindred delisted, taking Relax Gaming with it. Games Global withdrew its IPO. **The only pure-play listed slot-content comparables left are Evolution and Hacksaw**, plus L&W's iGaming segment and Playtech's B2B line. This is precisely why Pragmatic Play's opacity matters so much: the largest single content supplier by European GGR share has no public financials, and there is no listed peer of comparable shape to triangulate from.

### 6.4 Where Pragmatic Play is genuinely vulnerable

1. **Quality-per-release deficit.** 25.13 average points vs ELK's 206.4 and Hacksaw's 90.4. Volume strategies decay when operators start curating lobbies rather than filling them.
2. **PG Soft in Asia.** PG Soft leads the Philippines at 5.68% vs Pragmatic's 5.34% and ranks #2 globally on composite discovery traffic. Pragmatic has no equivalent mobile-first Asian architecture.
3. **The crash vertical.** Spribe owns it. Pragmatic has no meaningful crash asset; BGaming (Aviamasters) and TaDa are both further ahead in casual/crash formats.
4. **Live casino cost structure.** Expanding from 5 to 9 studios and 500 to 1,500+ tables, into a vertical where Evolution's revenue is declining and L&W has exited entirely.
5. **Franchise concentration.** Big Bass and Gates of Olympus carry the distribution footprint. Big Bass is Reel Kingdom content — a separate studio, however tightly bound.
6. **Regulatory exposure without disclosure.** Pragmatic withdrew from US sweepstakes in 2025. As a private company it faces the same UKGC/EU supply-chain enforcement risk that just cost Evolution £4.75m and a near-suspension, with no public visibility into its compliance posture.
