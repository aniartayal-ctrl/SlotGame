# Phase 1 — Decide your goal (30–60 minutes)

Do this on paper or in a notes app. Do not skip it. Clear goals save days of wasted training.

---

## Step 1: Pick ONE first project

Choose **only one** for your first LoRA:

### Option A — Symbol style (recommended first)
Goal: make reel icons (wilds, scatters, fruits, gems, sevens) look like *your* game.

### Option B — Character / mascot
Goal: make one hero character appear consistently (face, outfit, colors).

### Option C — Background / scene
Goal: generate bonus round backgrounds in your world.

### Option D — UI chrome
Goal: frames, buttons, panels, metallic borders.

**Recommendation:** start with **Option A**. Symbols are the heart of slots and are easier to keep consistent.

---

## Step 2: Write your goal in one sentence

Fill this in:

> I want the AI to generate **[asset type]** that look like **[describe style in 5–10 words]**, for use in **[game / studio / pitch]**.

Examples:

- “I want square reel symbols that look like glossy jeweled Egyptian gold, for Lucky Nile.”
- “I want neon cyberpunk fruit icons with thick outlines, for Neon Orchard.”
- “I want a friendly fox mascot in the same costume every time, for Fox Fortune.”

---

## Step 3: Choose your trigger word

Pick a short made-up word you will put in every caption and every future prompt.

Rules:

- One word or camelCase
- Not a normal English word like “gold” or “slot”
- Unique to you

Good examples:

- `slotartstyle`
- `nileGoldStyle`
- `foxFortuneLook`
- `reelJewelV1`

Write yours here:

> Trigger word: __________________

---

## Step 4: Decide what “good” means

Check the boxes that matter to you:

- [ ] Same color palette every time
- [ ] Same line thickness / outline style
- [ ] Same lighting (soft studio / hard neon / gold rim light)
- [ ] Centered on a clean background
- [ ] Readable at small reel size
- [ ] Matches an existing game Bible / style guide
- [ ] Works for many symbol types (not only gems)

Circle your top **3**. Those become your quality checklist later.

---

## Step 5: Decide what this LoRA should NOT do

Write 3 things you do **not** want:

1. _______________________________
2. _______________________________
3. _______________________________

Examples: “no photoreal faces”, “no busy backgrounds behind symbols”, “no text baked into icons”.

---

## Step 6: Inventory check

How many good example images do you already have for this one goal?

- [ ] Fewer than 15 → you need to create or commission more before training
- [ ] 15–40 → good for a first style LoRA
- [ ] 40–80 → excellent
- [ ] 100+ → great, but you may still start with a curated 40

**Hard truth:** 20 excellent, consistent images beat 200 messy ones.

---

You can answer yes to all:

- [ ] I picked one first project (A/B/C/D)
- [ ] I wrote one goal sentence
- [ ] I chose a trigger word
- [ ] I know my top 3 quality rules
- [ ] I know roughly how many images I have
